# Occult symbol kit — *Someone's Eating Babies*

Research gathered to give every illustration a deliberate esoteric layer. The principle (per user): wherever the AI generates an ornamental pattern-zone — the mirror frame, a temple dome, the mill's architecture — **replace the AI's gibberish detail with real, accurate symbols** that carry meaning and tie back to the dossier. Precise glyphs are unreliable from the image model (they come out as slop); render them as **vector overlays in the Portavec/SVG stage** so they're correct *and* clickable (each links to its timeline evidence record).

The payoff: the occult layer **is** the book's thesis — one continuous devouring entity wearing different masks across millennia. The symbols are not decoration; they are the argument.

## Per-scene placement

- **1 · the room** — mirror frame carved with the **Tree of Life (upright, intact)**; an **all-seeing eye** at the frame's crown; the clock face bears the **zodiac**; one small **Goetia sigil** hidden in the floorboards. Glass = obsidian, fog = smoke.
- **2 · the maxwells** (Mount of Olives) — the tomb stone cut with the **hexagram / Seal of Solomon**; the six mourners arranged like points of a seal; "cameras in mirrors" = the all-seeing eye as surveillance.
- **3 · the island** — the blue-striped temple's **gold dome = the eye**; "the dome yawned open: a door" → a **Goetic gateway sigil** on the dome's mouth; an **octopus/ouroboros** coiled around the island (the network); a hidden count of the 50 snares.
- **4 · the pyramid** — the **unfinished pyramid + Eye of Providence** of the Great Seal (the one-dollar-bill reverse) rising *round* the child and the mirror; the capstone eye = the **all-seeing eye**, rendered brazen-gold to seed **Moloch / Baal Hammon** of scenes 5–6; a **shore by the sea** glimpsed in the eye (Gaza, oblique — never named); the world below laid up as the **stones** of the pyramid (mass complicity as masonry); the dropped object = the **dollar** bearing the seal.
- **5 · the old altar** — **Moloch / Baal Hammon** full form: calf/bull-headed **brazen idol**, two horns, arms over the Tophet fire, brazen throne; **Yaldabaoth** (lion-headed serpent) rising in the smoke as "the dark thing."
- **6 · the new altar** — the same idol **hidden in the data center**: cooling towers = the **two horns**, the intake furnace = the idol's fire-mouth, "bright eyes / every face" = the **all-seeing eye**, "a thousand small hands" = the **octopus**. Moloch = "Lord of the Brazier" rendered literally as the brass mill.
- **7 · the telling** — the six gathered objects laid in a row, each paired with the **permission-symbol** the child refuses: **name** (true-name sigil), **sigh** (breath glyph), **mark** (Goetia seal), **vow** (pact scroll), **offering** (Tophet altar), **bow** (threshold). The mirror cracks to reveal the **full Qliphoth**, then dies as she refuses.
