# Someone's Eating Babies

A creepy/poetic children's book. Donaldson cadence (*Room on the Broom*) with Gorey/Mother Goose dread underneath. The mirror is established as a scrying device in scene **1** and then carried by the illustrations — the girl is pulled into the glass, ghost-of-Christmas-past style, and each subsequent scene is what the mirror is showing her. The text doesn't re-invoke the device after page 1; the pictures do.

**Structural engine** (Donaldson's accumulating-broom-friends pattern, transposed): between each scene a 2-line refrain returns (*"And the mirror went still..."*), and a small object drops at the child's feet. By scene 7 she has six small things laid out — and the burden she's gathered becomes the salvation she uses to refuse the entity.

**Refrain:** `Someone's eating babies.` Returns in scene **1** — once. The book trusts the line to echo on its own.

**Permission framework (scene 7's payoff):** Research across Slavic vampire folklore, Sethian Gnostic Archon theology, the *Faustbuch*, the Sinai covenant, the Lesser Key of Solomon, the Tophet inscriptions, and Christian deliverance-ministry literature yielded **four load-bearing conditions** the entity requires before it can enter a soul, plus two secondary ones:

1. **invitation / threshold-crossing** (Slavic vampire folklore + Briggs's fairy scholarship + Derek Prince)
2. **oath / covenant sworn** (Sinai → Faust → Goetia → generational-curse theology)
3. **offering given at the altar** (Tophet inscriptions + Leviticus + grimoire propitiation)
4. **interior assent / forgetting** (Sethian Gnostic Archon theology + Christian deliverance "open doors")
5. *the name spoken* (grimoire + Crowley + Exodus 23:13)
6. *the mark accepted* (Revelation 13 + Crowley sigils + Goetia)

The child learns these and refuses them. The book the reader holds is her telling.

## 1. the room

> *A child sat alone in a small quiet room,*
> *with a clock on the mantel and a broom in the gloom.*
> *On the wall hung a mirror in an old wooden frame —*
> *and the mirror it knew everyone's name.*

> *The mirror grew foggy. The mirror grew bright.*
> *It opened its glass to a far candlelight.*
> *"Quick," said the mirror. "Come quickly my child.*
> *Step into the dark where a land lies beguiled."*

> *She crossed through the frame, past the statehouses, banks,*
> *past presidents, senators, the courts and their ranks.*
> *Past temples and altars and back-doored machines,*
> *past a war won by guile — and the mockingbird screens.*

> *And under the noise of the world's great affairs,*
> *the mirror drew her down, down the back of the stairs,*
> *to the small ones held down, to be traded in pairs —*
> *"Someone's eating babies," the old mirror declares.*

- **L1–4** — the room. The child-witness is introduced sitting alone with two ordinary nursery-room objects (clock, broom) and the scrying device (mirror in old wooden frame). *The mirror it knew everyone's name* — the device's omniscience is the load-bearing claim; it is what allows scenes 2–6 to be *what the mirror shows her*. Mirror-scrying is the cross-traditional anchor (catoptromancy in Greek/Roman practice; *Mirror, mirror* in the Grimm corpus; Snow White's queen; the obsidian mirror in Aztec Tezcatlipoca cult; John Dee's black mirror) — the book does not name any of these, but the figure carries them.
- **L5–8** — the mirror activates. *Foggy* + *bright* is the scrying-glass coming alive. *Far candlelight* = the kitchen-table soft-candle load that the previous draft made explicit, now compressed into a single image and held at distance. *"Quick," said the mirror. "Come quickly my child"* drops the soothing/lullaby register for urgency — the mirror summons the child as witness, not soothes her. The vocative *my child* establishes the device-as-elder / witness-as-pupil framing that scenes 2–6 will inhabit: each scene is a guided tour through the mirror's omniscient view. *where a land lies beguiled* names the spell-state the book will catalogue across scenes 2–6, and widens the lens from a single soul to a captured polity — paid off immediately by the L9–12 montage, which names that land institution by institution (statehouses, banks, presidents, senators, courts, Washington's screens). The vague-then-specific move is the inverse of L1–2's specific-then-vague: the reader who knows the dossier hears *America*; the reader who doesn't hears *a land*. The present-tense *lies beguiled* holds the spell as an ongoing condition — the land is under it now, not merely once was.
- **L9–12** — the foreshadow montage. The mirror sweeps the geography of the beguilement before showing any single scene. *Statehouses* + *banks* anchor the regulatory and financial capture: the [27 US state anti-BDS laws](https://www.aclu.org/issues/free-speech/rights-protesters/anti-boycott-bills) Robert Steele names directly (`sources/captures/DSkvtpnjtDQ`), JPMorgan's [$290M Epstein settlement (2023)](https://www.reuters.com/legal/jpmorgan-reach-290-mln-settlement-with-jeffrey-epstein-accusers-2023-06-12/), Deutsche Bank's [$75M (2022)](https://www.reuters.com/legal/deutsche-bank-pays-75-mln-settle-claims-it-aided-jeffrey-epsteins-sex-trafficking-2022-05-18/), and the Bear Stearns "sheep-dip" placement Steele describes. *Presidents* = Clinton (~26 Lolita Express trips) + Trump (the Mar-a-Lago overlap, the 1992 NBC tape, multiple [black-book](https://www.epsteininvestigation.org/guides/epstein-black-book-explained) entries) — and per Steele the operation was taken "up to the presidential level". *Senators* = the named figures in the black book + the legislators who passed the anti-BDS bills. *The courts and their ranks* = [Acosta non-prosecution agreement (2008)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Acosta), [Berman SDNY firing (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Berman), the [cell-suicide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Jeffrey_Epstein), and Steele's claim that "all of them" — the 50 Mossad pedophilia entrapment operations he names — are "protected by the FBI". *Temples and altars and back-doored machines* foreshadows scenes 3 (the temple), 5/6 (the altars), and the appendix scene *the small doors* ([PROMIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inslaw), per Steele the prototype of "blackmail for back-door computer access"). *A war won by guile — and the mockingbird screens* is the central diptych of the book's argument, now carried by allusion rather than place-name. *A war won by guile* is the Mossad motto register — the [Proverbs 24:6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossad) verse on the service's seal ("for by wise guidance you shall wage your war"), rendered popularly as the title of [Victor Ostrovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Ostrovsky)'s defector memoir *By Way of Deception*; it carries the same load the place-name did (the [Mount of Olives funeral](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1991/11/11/israel-gives-maxwell-farewell-fit-for-hero/1773995a-0eac-4a3e-abed-1d3254cc0baa/), [PROMIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inslaw), the [Samson Option](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Samson_Option), [Vanunu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordechai_Vanunu)). *The mockingbird screens* is [Operation Mockingbird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Mockingbird) — the CIA's press penetration documented by the [Church Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_Committee) and [Carl Bernstein's 1977 *Rolling Stone* exposé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Mockingbird) — and hands directly into the next stanza's *under the noise of the world's great affairs* (the media cover Steele names); behind it sit the surveillance screens, the back-doored computers, and the data-center of scene 6.
- **L13–16** — the descent, and the refrain. After the global sweep (L9–12) the lens turns down, and the last three lines build a single falling motion — surface → hidden place → the children → the act. *Under the noise of the world's great affairs* = the cover that buries the dossier (Steele: *"the media today is not covering"*). *The mirror drew her down, down the back of the stairs* = the descent beneath the public world into the hidden architecture; *the back of the stairs* = the unseen servants'-stair. *Stairs* lifts from Mother Goose's *Goosey Goosey Gander* (*Upstairs, downstairs*) and the staircase at Maxwell's London house where the [2001 Andrew / Virginia Giuffre photograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Giuffre) was taken. *To the small ones held down, to be traded in pairs* = the trafficked children; *traded in pairs* is [Maxwell's convicted paired-recruitment pattern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_of_Ghislaine_Maxwell) and **seeds scene 2's *She brought them in pairs*** — page 1 plants the word page 2 pays off. *Someone's eating babies, the old mirror declares* — the refrain debut, named but not shown: the mirror cuts the vision before the horror fully materializes (the showing is scenes 2–6). *Old* flags the mirror's continuity with the *long, long ago* of scene 5 (the device that watched Carthage); the present-tense *declares* matches the ongoing present of the crime. AAAA monorhyme on *-airs* (affairs / stairs / pairs / declares) replaces the earlier soft beds/said slant.

> *And the mirror went still. And the mirror went grey.*
> *A small empty shoe lay at her feet at the dawn of the day.*

## 2. the maxwells

> *And the stairs she came down now turned over and rose,*
> *up out of the dark to a hill the world knows —*
> *where a man had been carried and laid down to sleep*
> *in a tomb of pale stone that was windy and steep.*
> *Six men in dark coats stood by him to weep —*
> *six men whose old debts to the dead man ran deep.*

> *Then the mirror breathed close: "His daughter would glide*
> *in a small silver sub through the cold and the tide.*
> *She counted the children. She logged every name.*
> *And it was me on the wall — I caught them in frame."*

> *"She brought them in pairs, but she ran, did not own.*
> *The dead man had built it. The masters? Unknown.*
> *She kept all the keys — but she answered to more,*
> *to the hands up the stair that my faces were for."*

- **L1–2** — the bridge from scene 1, in anapestic tetrameter to match the Donaldson cadence. *And the stairs she came down now turned over and rose / up out of the dark* picks up scene 1's closing descent (*the mirror drew her down, down the back of the stairs*) and inverts it — the staircase she went *down* now *turns over* and *rose*s out of the ground at the burial hill. This is the scrying-device's dream-transport (cf. scene 1's *the mirror drew her down* / *it opened its glass*), not a factual claim; *turned over* carries the SYMBOL-KIT's occult inversion — the **Tree of Life flipping to the Qliphoth** (the Tree of Death) as the vision darkens. *a hill the world knows* lands the reader at the Mount of Olives (L3–4): the place is left unnamed so the image lands first, but the earlier *a hill no one knows* was cut as false — the Mount of Olives is one of the most sacred and most-recognized burial grounds on earth (the reader who knows the dossier sees Jerusalem; the reader who doesn't sees a famous hill). *The world knows* the hill — that it holds Maxwell among Israel's honored dead is the buried point.
- **L3–4** — the burial site: [Robert Maxwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Maxwell)'s tomb on the [Mount of Olives](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1991/11/11/israel-gives-maxwell-farewell-fit-for-hero/1773995a-0eac-4a3e-abed-1d3254cc0baa/) — Maxwell drowned off his yacht the *Lady Ghislaine* on **November 5, 1991**, and was buried there — one of Judaism's most sacred burial sites — on **November 10, 1991**. The Mount of Olives is limestone; *pale stone* is literal. *laid down to sleep* echoes scene 1's *the small ones held down*.
- **L5** — per [Gordon Thomas, *Gideon's Spies*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Maxwell) and corroborating press, **six serving and former heads of Israeli intelligence** attended. The wider published roster includes PM Yitzhak Shamir, President Chaim Herzog, Shimon Peres, and Natan Sharansky; the six intelligence figures are the buried-headline.
- **L6** — *Old debts to the dead man ran deep* anchors the Maxwell–Israel-intelligence relationship: per Thomas and [Seymour Hersh's *The Samson Option*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Samson_Option), Maxwell brokered the back-doored [PROMIS software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inslaw) for Mossad use, tipped Israeli intelligence on the [Vanunu leak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordechai_Vanunu) in 1986, and ran cross-border currency operations.
- **L7–8** — the mirror comes back. *Then the mirror breathed close* hands the stanza to the scrying device of scene 1 (the one that *knew everyone's name*), which now narrates the dead man's daughter in the first person — stanzas 2–3 become one continuous mirror monologue, the device-as-elder / witness-as-pupil framing scene 1 set. The referent: [Ghislaine Maxwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghislaine_Maxwell) was a [licensed submersible pilot](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/07/02/nyregion/ghislaine-maxwell-arrested-jeffrey-epstein.html), trained on the **Triton 1000-2** deep-sea sub Epstein owned at Little St James; *small silver sub* is literal, and *small silver* seeds the *small silver camera* of this scene's closer.
- **L9** — [Maxwell was convicted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_of_Ghislaine_Maxwell) in December 2021 on **five of six federal counts** including sex trafficking of a minor; sentenced to **20 years** in June 2022. *She logged every name* tightens the language to compress her bookkeeping role.
- **L10** — *And it was me on the wall — I caught them in frame* is the reveal: the mirror admits in the first person that it *is* the surveillance glass. *on the wall* anchors the confession to scene 1's *On the wall hung a mirror in an old wooden frame* — the device that hangs on the wall is the watcher. Per [Maria Farmer's testimony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Giuffre), Sarah Ransome's published account, and the [FBI CHS memo EFTA00090314](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/files/DataSet%209/EFTA00090314.pdf), Epstein's properties were laced with surveillance — including two-way mirrors with cameras documented in the Palm Beach house. The scrying device the child looks through in scene 1 (the mirror that *knew everyone's name*) is here collapsed onto the literal blackmail apparatus — the two-way mirror that *caught faces in frame*. *frame* carries the mirror's wooden frame, the camera frame, and the blackmail "framing" at once.
- **L11** — *brought them in pairs* holds the seeded thread (scene 1's *traded in pairs* → scene 3's *two by two*): [Maxwell's convicted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_of_Ghislaine_Maxwell) paired-recruitment pattern. *she ran, did not own* demotes her from the omniscient controller the prior stanza made her — she was the convicted operator and recruiter, not the proprietor (the houses, planes, island, and money were Epstein's; the operation's origin sat above her). This rework pivots the close of scene 2 from Maxwell-as-apex to the operators-served-masters thesis the book already carries — Steele, `sources/captures/DSkvtpnjtDQ`: the operation "reached the presidential level."
- **L12** — *the dead man had built it* keeps the fixed epithet (Robert Maxwell, set in this scene's first stanza) and names him as the network's origin: per [Ari Ben-Menashe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Ben-Menashe) — former Israeli intelligence officer, *Epstein: Dead Men Tell No Tales* and the 2019 Narativ / Zev Shalev interview — Maxwell was the confirmed asset who brought Epstein and Ghislaine into an intelligence-linked blackmail operation in the 1980s. **This is Ben-Menashe's contested account, not an established finding** — his credibility is disputed and no documentary proof has been produced; the verse renders it as the mirror's vision and does not name the master. *the masters unknown* = the patrons above were never named or charged (Steele: the operations are "all of them protected by the FBI"); leaving them unnamed is the honest calibration and seeds scene 4 (the protected orbit).
- **L13** — *she kept all the keys* compresses her bookkeeping role (the calendars, address books, property-staff schedules) and picks up the keys / *small doors* motif. *she answered to more* is the pawn statement — subordinate, not apex.
- **L14** — *to the hands up the stair that my faces were for* keeps the mirror's first person from L10 (*my faces* = the faces it caught in frame) and threads the surveillance beat forward. *up the stair* reconnects the staircase motif — scene 1's *the back of the stairs*, this scene's inverted bridge (L1–2) — so the harvested faces travel *up* to the unnamed *hands*. [Maria Farmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Farmer) testified that Epstein's "media room" — cameras trained on the beds — was equipment she believes was used to "create leverage over powerful people" (her Aug/Sept 1996 NYPD + FBI report, now in the DOJ release; cf. [FBI CHS memo EFTA00090314](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/files/DataSet%209/EFTA00090314.pdf)). The product goes *up* — per Ben-Menashe, "to suit Israeli intelligence interests"; per Steele, the Mossad "blackmails corporate chiefs and others… to plant back doors." The apparatus is here revealed as a service rendered to masters above the operators — the "servicing masters" pivot, and the setup for scenes 3 (one snare of fifty) and 4 (the protected orbit). **Sascha Riley's viral 2025 audio (uncorroborated, no court/police/Epstein-Files confirmation) informed the thematic direction but is deliberately not a referent for any line.**

> *And the mirror went still. And the mirror went grey.*
> *A small silver camera lay at her feet at the dawn of the day.*

## 3. the island

> *On an island at sea where no stranger could land,*
> *a blue-striped white temple stood gold on the sand.*
> *A plane slid in low with no lights and no name,*
> *and the children stepped down, two by two, as they came.*

> *An old man was waiting. An old man kept score.*
> *He opened the dance, and he bolted the door.*
> *Round and round by the temple, and no one let go —*
> *for to loosen a hand was to fall, and to show.*

> *There's a man with a saxophone, charm from the South;*
> *there's a builder with gold towers and a loud, boasting mouth;*
> *there's a merchant who made angels to sell to the world;*
> *there's a maker of windows where secrets unfurled.*

> *There's a general who governed a land by the sea;*
> *there's a lawyer who swore he was clean as could be;*
> *and each held the next by a thread of their shame —*
> *fifty rings round the world, and they all knew the game.*

> *For the threads all run up to one hand in the dark,*
> *that can lift them or drop them like toys in a park.*
> *And the dance never stops. And the truth was not told.*
> *And not one ring was broken. And the file stays cold.*

The scene was reworked (per user direction) from a three-stanza vignette to a five-stanza danse macabre: momentum, a dance that cannot be stopped, the elites all holding each other's compromise and all strung from one hand above — the puppet-master thread from scene 2. The island guests are alluded to **obliquely** — each line names no one and describes a documented association the reader is meant to look up. Several of these people deny the allegations against them; the verse asserts nothing specific and draws no conclusion.

The roll-call gives each figure their **documented estate** — *builder* (Trump, developer), *merchant* (Wexner, retailer), *maker of windows* (Gates, Microsoft), *general* (Barak, IDF Chief of Staff), *lawyer* (Dershowitz) — rather than a repeated *"a man."* This is the medieval danse-macabre form proper (Death's procession of the estates: king, merchant, physician, lawyer…), and it converts the flat repetition into a second documented fact per line. Clinton leads as *"a man with a saxophone"* — the deliberately minimal framing (the saxophone is the only tell; the line points at the man, not a trade that would imply an act).

- **L1–2** — [Little Saint James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Saint_James), Epstein's **70-acre** private island in the U.S. Virgin Islands. *Where no stranger could land* = controlled-access privacy (guests flown or ferried in; no public landing). The [structure](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/jeffrey-epstein-s-bizarre-blue-striped-building-private-island-raised-n1037511): a white cube with **eight blue stripes** and a **gold dome** (added 2013–2014; blown off in Hurricane Maria, 2017), officially a "music pavilion." *Stood gold on the sand* = the gold dome — rendered in scene3a's art as the all-seeing eye (SYMBOL-KIT).
- **L3** — the [Lolita Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein), Epstein's Boeing 727 (registration **N908JE**).
- **L4** — *two by two* = the paired-recruitment pattern [Maxwell was convicted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_of_Ghislaine_Maxwell) on, held over from scenes 1–2; the Noah's-ark cadence heightens the nursery register.
- **L5** — *An old man… kept score* = Epstein, keeper of the [little black book](https://www.epsteininvestigation.org/guides/epstein-black-book-explained) (**97 pages, 1,500+ names**). *Kept score* = the ledger.

- **L7–8** — *Round and round… no one let go / for to loosen a hand was to fall, and to show* = the mutual-blackmail web: each guest is compromised, so to defect or expose another is to expose yourself — a danse macabre that cannot stop. The leverage is documented: [Maria Farmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Farmer) testified the surveillance was to "create leverage over powerful people" (cf. [EFTA00090314](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/files/DataSet%209/EFTA00090314.pdf)).
- **L9** — *a man with a saxophone, charm from the South* → **Bill Clinton**, oblique. The referent is the documented flight-log record on Epstein's jet; the saxophone + the South are the look-it-up tells. **Clinton denies visiting the island itself** — the line points at the *planes*, not an act on the sand, which is the defensible framing.
- **L10** — *a builder with gold towers and a loud, boasting mouth* → **Donald Trump**, oblique — *builder* is the literal estate (real-estate developer; the gold-toned [Trump Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Tower)); the 1990s–2000s social orbit, [black-book](https://www.epsteininvestigation.org/guides/epstein-black-book-explained) entries, the 1992 NBC Mar-a-Lago party footage. The money behind the operation. **Wexner says Epstein misappropriated his funds.**

- **L20** — *the truth was not told / not one ring was broken / the file stays cold* = institutional non-coverage (Steele: *"the media today is not covering"*), impunity (of the *fifty rings* of L16 none were broken or charged — Steele: *"all of them protected by the FBI"*), and the cold case (Epstein died pre-trial, co-conspirators largely uncharged, the island [sold for $60M in May 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Saint_James)).

## 4. the pyramid

> *The hand drew its threads up and out of the day,*
> *and the room pulled apart, and the walls fell away.*
> *A pyramid rose round the child and the glass,*
> *with an eye in its cap made of gold and of brass.*

> *And the eye, looking down, showed a shore by the sea*
> *where the children fell thin as the leaves off a tree.*
> *By thousands they fell — and the whole world could see.*
> *And the whole world looked on. And it let the eye be.*

> *But the thing at the top cannot enter alone.*
> *It must wait to be asked. It must wait to be shown*
> *it is wanted, and welcomed, and given its bread.*
> *So the world sent its coin. And the world bowed its head.*

> *For as it is above, so it is below:*
> *every wage, every tax, every yes makes it grow.*
> *A coin in the plate and a name on the deed,*
> *and each was a stone. And the stone made it feed.*

> *And the more that it ate, the more wide it would yawn:*
> *more tolls, and more taxes, and more children drawn.*
> *The eye never blinked, and the eye never tired,*
> *and the more that we fed it, the more it required.*

The scene turns the gaze **up** — following scene 3's *the threads all run up to one hand in the dark* — into the apex itself.
