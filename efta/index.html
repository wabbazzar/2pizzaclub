    $15.5M+ · pro-Israel PAC spend against Massie
    
                            
    Three pro-Israel-aligned super PACs poured a combined $15.5M+ into the May 2026 Kentucky 4th-district Republican primary against U.S. Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY): $4.1M from AIPAC's United Democracy Project, $3.9M from the RJC Victory Fund (Republican Jewish Coalition), and $7.5M from MAGA KY — itself funded by Paul Singer and Miriam Adelson's Preserve America PAC.
    
                            
    — Al Jazeera, "Massie race breaks spending record as pro-Israel groups target Trump critic," May 18, 2026.

    Why him · the discharge petition
    
                            
    Massie co-led the bipartisan House discharge petition to compel the Justice Department's release of the Epstein files and was openly critical of unconditional U.S. military aid to Israel and of Israel's conduct in Gaza and Lebanon. He called the race "a referendum on foreign policy" and asked whether pro-Israel lobby groups could "bully" members of Congress.
    
                            
    — The Intercept, "Rep. Thomas Massie Loses His Seat in a Win for Trump — and AIPAC," May 19, 2026.

    $32M+ · the most expensive U.S. House primary in history
    
                            
    Massie lost to Trump-endorsed former Navy SEAL Ed Gallrein on May 19, 2026, by roughly 55%–45%. Total race spending hit $32M+ (AdImpact). Outside super-PAC spending favored the challenger $16.4M to $10.1M; Massie's own campaign outspent Gallrein's $5.8M to $2.6M.
    
                            
    — Washington Post, "Thomas Massie loses to Trump-endorsed Ed Gallrein in Kentucky GOP primary," May 19, 2026.
