EFTA findings.

Statistical extraction from documents released under the Epstein Files Transparency Act (DOJ Data Sets 1–12 and the House Oversight estate release). Names ranked by mention count. N-gram phrases ranked by repetition. A small PII model (iiiorg/piiranha-v1-detect-personal-information) tags entities; the counts are what you see.

What appears here is what the model found, not what we picked. Re-runs as more of the release lands locally; the corpus and date are stamped on each finding.
