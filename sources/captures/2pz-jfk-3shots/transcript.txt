[Sworn testimony of Douglas P. Horne — former Chief Analyst for Military Records, Assassination Records Review Board (ARRB) — to the U.S. House Oversight Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets, "The JFK Files" hearing, May 20, 2025.]

Two highly qualified and respected MDs, who were granted repeated access by the Kennedy family to the autopsy photographs and x-rays in the deed of gift collection at the archives, both agree that the extant JFK skull x-rays reveal unambiguous and clear evidence of two head shots fired from in front of the president, contrary to the findings of the Warren Commission and the HSCA. The JFK skull x-rays actually reveal a total of three head shots, one from behind and two from the front.
